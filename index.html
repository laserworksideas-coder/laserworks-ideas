ABOUT US.

EST. 2013

Laserworks Ideas is a creative laser cutting studio in Limassol, Cyprus, specialising in custom laser cut MDF, wood and acrylic products. We also offer laser engraving and laser craft, creating personalised gifts, names, signs, decorations and custom designs for christenings, birthdays, weddings, schools and events.

Our services include personalised clothing printing, custom event decorations and unique gift details such as balloons, soaps, candy and cake decorations.

We also create custom corporate gifts and work with event planners and businesses. We deliver throughout Cyprus.
