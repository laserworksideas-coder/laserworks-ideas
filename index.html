ABOUT LASERWORKS IDEAS

Laser Cutting & Engraving
in Limassol, Cyprus

Laserworks Ideas is a creative laser cutting and engraving studio based in Limassol, Cyprus, specialising in custom MDF, wood, plywood and acrylic products. We create personalised gifts, names, signs, decorations and custom designs for christenings, birthdays, weddings, schools, events and businesses.

Our services also include personalised clothing printing, custom event decorations, balloons, soaps, candy and cake decorations, as well as corporate gifts for businesses and event planners.

Based in Limassol, we serve customers throughout Cyprus.
