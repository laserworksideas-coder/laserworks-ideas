ABOUT LASERWORKS IDEAS

Laserworks Ideas is a creative laser cutting and engraving studio based in Limassol, Cyprus, specialising in custom-made MDF, wood and acrylic creations.

We combine precision, creativity and attention to detail to create personalised gifts, names, signs, decorations and unique custom designs for christenings, birthdays, weddings, schools, events and special occasions.

Alongside laser cutting and engraving, we offer personalised clothing printing, custom event decorations and a wide range of unique gift details, including balloons, soaps, candy and cake decorations.

We also create bespoke corporate gifts and collaborate with event planners, businesses and professionals to bring their ideas to life.

Whether you are looking for a personalised gift, a custom decoration or a complete creative solution for your event, we are here to turn your idea into something special.

Based in Limassol • Delivering throughout Cyprus
